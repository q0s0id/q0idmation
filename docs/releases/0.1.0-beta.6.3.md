# q0idmation v0.1.0-beta.6.3

beta.6.3 brings Trace Bitmap, a new colour picker with a Color panel, a camera for every scene, and a reworked pen, eraser and eyedropper. Settings and every dialog get the new look. Selecting vector art no longer slows the editor down, and a long list of nitpicks from artists is fixed.

## trace bitmap

- Modify > Trace Bitmap turns a bitmap on the stage into vector shapes, one per colour, placed where the bitmap was. Also on the stage's right-click menu and in Properties.
- choose up to 255 colours, the opacity cutoff, the smallest patch kept, how closely curves follow the pixels (0 keeps exact pixel edges, for pixel art) and how sharp corners stay. The swatches show the colours you will get.
- neighbouring colours meet without cracks or hairlines. one undo brings the bitmap back.

## colour

- a new colour picker in every colour box: old | new, hex, a square or a hue wheel, sliders, harmonies, recent and saved swatches and the project's own colours. its eyedropper picks from anywhere on screen, other windows too.
- a Color panel with fill and stroke swatches, the wheel, and "none" for either.
- the Eyedropper tool copies vector paint exactly, takes the colour shown anywhere else (bitmaps, gradients, the background), and picks from anywhere on screen when you drag from the stage. Shift picks into the stroke.

## drawing

- the pen: click for a corner, drag for a curve (Alt moves one handle, Shift keeps 45°), click the first point to close, Enter or a double-click to end, Backspace to take a point back. the path shows as it will be drawn.
- the eraser has the brush's controls: nib, size, smoothing, pressure, speed and minimum size. synced, it is the brush exactly; otherwise it keeps its own settings.
- the text tool starts the line on the click, not under it.
- lines are cut where they cross, so each piece can be picked on its own (turn off in the Line or Pen options). snapping shows a ring where it snaps.
- stroke Corners works on a clicked side of an outline.
- the selection marquee picks whole shapes and lines, as Shift-clicking each would, so Properties edits them. Alt-drag cuts out only the part inside, as before.

## faster

- selecting vector art no longer makes the editor crawl: the GPU kept only four cached meshes and uploaded the whole scene again every frame while something was selected.
- a selected stroke shows a clean dotted body with no stray lines across it.

## timeline and scenes

- every scene has its own camera, in the editor, in playback and in exports. Fast Preview and export of one scene use that scene's camera.
- the scene list fits the Properties panel and its buttons line up.
- F6 while dragging objects carries every layer on to the new frame.
- onion skin shows every unlocked layer, as in Animate. lock a layer to leave it out, or untick "all unlocked".
- a mask layer's preview is saved with the project.
- online avatars never take a row from the timeline; whoever does not fit is under a "+N" button.

## the editor's look

- Settings is split into pages with a search, and the shortcut editor lives in it. every window and dialog shares the new look and fades in.
- Properties has tabs for the selection, the tool and the stage, and switches to what you touched. a tool's shortcut shows by its name, to change it right there.
- drawings sit in Graphics folders at the end of the library, one per timeline, and unused ones are no longer saved.
- the library remembers what each open project was showing, and the arrow keys move through it.

## file compatibility

projects saved by beta.6.3 can use two new formats: a mask previewing its clipping, and a camera on a scene after the first. **beta.6.2 and earlier cannot open those**, and only beta.6.3's q0player plays their .q0s movies. other projects stay readable by beta.6.2. beta.6.3 opens every older project.
