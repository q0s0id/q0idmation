# q0idmation v0.1.0-beta.5.1

beta.5.1 adds lights, particles, liquids and ropes, runs physics and particles live in q0player, and fixes a long list of fla imports. strokes get square ends and chosen corners, rotation tweens turn around their anchor, and the editor stays responsive with heavy masks.

## lights

an opt-in tool: settings > extra tools > light editor. lights a project already has keep shining while it is off.

- point lights and spotlights that really light the stage: what is drawn under the light layer is multiplied by the light reaching it, so a lamp shows the colours under it instead of washing them out. layers above the light layer, a hud for example, stay as drawn.
- darkness and an ambient colour per timeline: deep blue for night, black for a cave.
- colour, brightness, reach and softness; spotlights add direction, cone width and a soft cone edge. glow adds a halo, and flicker makes a light waver.
- lights can ride along with an object and are keyframed with easing.
- nothing is baked: the editor, exports and q0player light every frame on the gpu.

## particles

an opt-in tool: settings > extra tools > particle system.

- emitters spray fire, smoke, sparks, explosions, magic, galaxies, portals, snow, rain, confetti, bubbles, fireflies, fountains, electricity, or copies of a library item.
- simple mode starts from a preset with the settings that matter most. pro mode shows every setting with its ± variance, and size, spin and colour run from a start to an end value over each particle's life.
- particles are baked into ordinary keyframes on a layer of their own, with their drawings in a library folder of their own, so a bake can be redone without touching anything else.

## physics

- liquids: streams of particles that flow, splash, hold together in drops and push floating bodies around. thickness, stiffness and stickiness are settings.
- links: ropes, chains, springs, hinges and welds tie one object to another. ropes and chains wobble along as their objects move; length, stiffness, damping and a breaking force are settings, and a link can be drawn as a plain line, a chain or a coil.
- hull and outline colliders follow an object's real artwork. an outline follows a drawn line instead of filling the space around it.
- the solver runs at a simulation rate in hz, so the same settings behave the same at any frame rate. canvas-edge collisions can be turned off or limited to chosen sides.
- previews run on a thread of their own, so a long range or a big liquid never freezes the editor.

## q0player and q0lang

- worlds and emitters marked live travel in the movie, and q0lang drives them while it plays: `q0physics.start!`, `push!`, `teleport!`, `gravity!`, `cut!` and more, and `q0particles.start!`, `stop!`, `burst!`, `move!` and `clear!`.
- `q0light.on!`, `off!`, `move!`, `color!`, `brightness!`, `size!` and `darkness!` change lights at runtime.
- the q0lang documentation is reorganized, with a page of recipes and a downloadable guide.

## drawing and animation

- strokes can end round, flat or square, and turn their corners round, sharp or cut. very sharp corners are cut so shaky freehand lines cannot grow spikes.
- a selected straight line has grips on both ends: drag one to turn and stretch the line; shift keeps it at multiples of 45 degrees.
- motion tweens turn around the object's transform anchor, so a rotation around a fixed point stays fixed instead of swinging along a chord. numeric rotation in properties uses the same anchor.
- artwork can live anywhere: the paper sets the output size, not where you can draw. shapes can start off the stage.
- the text tool's caret lands exactly where the glyphs are drawn, the text box hugs its text, and on touch screens a box under the on-screen keyboard moves into view.

## fla import

- groups no longer carry their position twice, which threw artwork far off its place.
- symbols resolve by their real library names, including names with stray spaces, so objects are no longer swapped.
- bitmap fills, rectangle and oval primitives, jpeg bitmaps, variable-width strokes, stroke ends and corners, and brightness tints import instead of disappearing.
- legacy binary fla text imports with its fonts and colours, so scenes with text no longer lose everything after it.
- documents over 64 mb and libraries of tens of thousands of items import. drawings that do not fit the project's limits are reported instead of dropped silently.
- a mask layer locked in the fla opens locked with its mask preview on, so it clips as it did in animate. guide layers stay hidden, and buttons import as single-frame symbols.

## performance and fixes

- heavy masks the gpu cannot cut are now drawn on a background thread. the stage keeps the last picture while the new one renders instead of freezing on every change.
- characters the interface fonts lack, such as chinese, japanese, korean and symbols, show through system fonts instead of boxes.
- window and menu shadows on dark themes were nearly invisible because of an overflow; they show again.
- some things are only found by those who knock on the right door.

## file compatibility

the current readers support **q1s v42** and **q0s v41**. projects are saved in the oldest version that can hold what they use: only projects with particles, liquids, links, live physics or transform anchors need beta.5.1. update both applications; older builds reject files that use the new features.
