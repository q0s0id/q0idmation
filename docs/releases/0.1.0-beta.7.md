# q0idmation v0.1.0-beta.7

beta.7 adds raster animation layers to q0editor, reworks how vector edits settle into the drawing, and brings a compact phone interface to the q0id browser player.

## raster animation

- add a raster layer from the timeline and draw pixels directly into keyframes. ordinary holds, blank keyframes, onion skin, masks, symbols and export work with raster drawings.
- choose from pencils, ink, paint, charcoal and airbrush presets. the raster toolbox also includes erasers, smudge, soften, fill, colour picking and pixel selections.
- keep transparency to recolour painted areas, paint behind existing strokes, and use ink on other raster layers as the boundary for a fill.
- use the rectangle or lasso to lift and transform part of a raster drawing. copy and paste pixels between frames.
- raster drawings save as compressed tiles, with repeated tiles stored once. raster layers are available in local projects. online rooms still use vector layers.

[read the raster layers guide](https://github.com/q0s0id/q0idmation/blob/v0.1.0-beta.7/docs/raster-layers.md)

## drawing and selection

- moving selected raw artwork defers merging until deselection. a stroke crossed by a moved fill is split when the edit settles.
- disconnected fill pieces and the bounds of cut pieces follow their actual geometry. click a fill to pick it alone and double-click to select its adjoining strokes.
- the marquee tests painted geometry and clipping masks. the outline around the selection still shows its transform bounds.
- subselect can delete several selected points together. the knife and eraser can cut a stroke into pieces.
- close gaps closes short breaks with straight joins. pen double-clicks are checked against the click position.

## timeline, effects and workspace

- click the ruler to jump to a frame. click the current frame to select that frame’s objects on its layer.
- layers have opacity and effects. a 2d outline is available on layers and symbol instances, including artwork under masks.
- the transform box and tool cursors have a new look. the editor also has a new project dialog and more timeline appearance controls.

## editor performance

- undo keeps the geometry and bounds for unchanged artwork cached.
- onion-skin rendering reuses cached effects across the live frame and ghost frames.
- mp4 export reuses the frame renderer and lets up to eight workers prepare frames while keeping encoder input in timeline order.

## q0player and q0id

- phones and narrow embeds get a one-row transport with play/pause, seek, time and fullscreen. restart, frame stepping and loop are in the extra controls menu. a tap keeps the controls visible for five seconds, and they stay visible during a scrub or while paused.
- gradients are sampled by a shader. the player composites a fading symbol as a group on the gpu, including its masks, and reuses vertex buffers for unchanged artwork. this addresses the lamp and logo fade in the cell adventures pilot.

## downloads

- android arm64 has a separate apk for each app.
