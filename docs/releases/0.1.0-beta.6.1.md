# q0idmation v0.1.0-beta.6.1

beta.6.1 reworks vector drawing, packs rooms and project files so drawings stop hitting the room limit, and works through everything people ran into in beta.6's first rooms: layer locks, the timeline bar, sounds, tweens, the camera, the code editors and the phone menu. plugins can reach much further, q0editor remembers your q0id sign-in, and Discord can show what you are working on.

## drawing

- lines drawn through a point where others meet no longer multiply into millions of pieces and freeze the editor. crossing lines are cut at one shared point, and scraps too small to see are dropped.
- closed shapes keep their fill and outline apart: the fill is paint that joins paint of its colour, the outline is a line other lines cut. drawing one shape over another no longer melts their outlines into one and deletes the lines you wanted.
- the bucket fills closed shapes made of crossing lines, and lines that only overlap fill as they look. the bucket's gap setting starts at small.
- brush paint modes: normal, behind, inside and over fills.
- the line tool's closed shapes fill, and it no longer crashes.

## layers and the timeline

- a lock keeps a layer's artwork safe, not its frames, as in Animate: keyframes, frames, tweens, moving and pasting cells and sounds all work on locked layers. in a room, layers you may only watch still refuse them.
- lock all and hide all remember what they turned on and give the rest back. shift-click clears all, and dragging down the eye or lock column sets every row it passes in one undo step.
- alt-click a layer's lock, eye or outline to set every other layer. a plain click on that same switch gives them back, as long as none of them was changed since.
- the timeline bar is one row: layer and frame buttons on the left, playback in the middle, the view on the right. its ☰ menu switches any button on or off.
- ctrl+wheel over the frames makes them wider or narrower. drag a row's bottom edge in the layer names to resize every row; alt-drag resizes one layer, and a double-click puts it back.
- sound clips drag along the timeline and onto other layers, and their ends trim them. a sound dropped on the stage starts at the playhead, on a layer of its own when it does not fit.

## tweens and the camera

- a keyframe made inside a motion or camera tween splits it and carries the rest on instead of stopping dead.
- tween settings have a grid of curves, an ease slider, which way and how many times to turn, and the motion path drawn on the stage.
- space and the middle button pan the view, never the camera. a corner of the camera frame zooms it; just outside one turns it.
- the free 3D camera looks from the middle of the canvas, so turning and zooming no longer happen off to one side when the 2D view was panned.

## workspace

- panels collapse to an icon strip at the right edge (tab menu > collapse to icon). an icon opens its panel beside the strip, a click elsewhere closes it, and **<<** docks them all again.
- **F9** shows only the stage; **F11** is borderless full screen. the q0lang editor moved to **alt+F9**.
- the online window no longer grows itself taller every frame.

## online

- rooms and project files are packed. drawings used to reach the 64 mb room limit at a few mb on disk; a room now holds up to 384 mb of project, sent and stored at a fraction of that. one project went from 66 mb to 15 mb.
- duplicate symbol and cross-project imports no longer copy every video and sound. identical media is kept once on load, duplicate and import; one project held the same 43 mb video 91 times.
- an upload too big for q0id says so instead of "can't reach the q0id server".
- q0editor remembers your q0id sign-in and signs back in at start. on windows it is sealed with your windows login; signing out forgets it.
- the editor says that joining with a room code makes you a viewer.

## plugins

- timeline words: `layer.keyframe!`, `blank!`, `clear!`, `add_frames!`, `remove_frames!`, `tween!`, `project.label!` and `project.labels`, `editor.layer` and `editor.cells`. they run the editor's own commands, so they behave like its buttons.
- `when action` runs after any editor command; `when stage_menu`, `layer_menu`, `frame_menu` and `library_menu` add items to the right-click menus; `plugin.send!` and `when message` let plugins talk to each other.
- a `key` setting lets people pick a plugin's shortcut themselves, and `when key "@name"` runs on it.
- `obj.paths` reads and reshapes drawings point by point; `project.import!` and `project.place!` bring files in and onto the stage; `file.pick`, `pick_save` and `pick_folder` ask with the system's dialogs; `draw.arrow!` and `draw.image!` draw over the stage.
- the plugin studio and the q0lang editor edit like code editors: backspace in an indent goes back a whole step, tab and shift+tab shift every selected line, ctrl+shift+k deletes a line, and an emptied line can be deleted. discard in the unsaved-changes question works for plugins.
- a new example plugin, keys, puts timeline helpers in the right-click menus.

## Discord

- with the Discord app open, your profile shows the project you are editing, the frame you are on and the size of your room. settings > discord turns it off or leaves the project's name out.

## phones and tablets

- the main menu scrolls, and its menus fold open in place, so nothing runs off the screen.
- online is in the main menu, and the q0id sign-in page opens in the phone's browser.

## icons and installers

- new icons for q0editor and q0player, document icons for .q0s, .q0v, .q1s and .q0l files, a CanRipper logo and redrawn android launcher icons.
- the editor installer opens .q0l and .q0lang files too.

## file compatibility

projects are saved packed, a fraction of their old size. beta.6.1 reads every older project, but **beta.6 and earlier cannot open projects saved by beta.6.1**, so update both applications. rooms need beta.6.1 for projects too big for beta.6.
