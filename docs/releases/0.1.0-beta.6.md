# q0idmation v0.1.0-beta.6

beta.6 brings q0editor online: shared project rooms where several people draw in one project at once, with chat, direct messages and roles. it also adds 3D models and scenes, plugins written in q0ex, gradients on every fill, spriting tools such as the knife, snapping and variants, shortcuts that work on any keyboard layout, and Windows on ARM and Android downloads on q0id.com.

## online rooms

online needs a q0id.com account. sign in from the editor once; it remembers the device.

- add friends, invite them to a room and draw together in the same project. strokes on the same layer, new symbols and reordered layers merge instead of overwriting each other; only the same setting changed by two people at once is reported as a conflict.
- ranks (viewer, editor, admin, owner) decide who may edit what: everything, or only the layers, layer folders and library folders the owner assigns. layers you can't edit show as locked.
- undo and redo are personal: they take back your own steps and keep other people's later work.
- everyone in a room has a colour. their current frame and layer light up on the timeline, their pictures sit on the timeline bar (click one to jump to where they are), and what someone just changed is outlined on the stage with their name.
- locking and outlining a layer in a room changes only your own view; hiding a layer is still shared.
- **who may save a copy** (room settings): everyone, editors and up (the default), admins and the owner, or only the owner. below that rank q0editor does not save, export or copy layers, frames or artwork out of the room, and keeps no recovery copy of it.
- room chat floats over the canvas like a game chat and fades away. `/` opens it, `/r` replies, `/w` whispers. the online panel has chat history and direct messages.
- chat lines and messages can carry pictures, gifs and videos. right-click a line or message to report it to the moderators.

## 3D

- model, rig and animate 3D models in the model editor, or import FBX, glTF/GLB and OBJ files with their materials, textures, bones and animations.
- use models as 3D pieces in a 2D scene, or build a fully 3D scene with a moving camera. 3D lights light models and 2D drawings alike, and suns and spotlights cast shadows onto both.
- glass materials, 3D physics with baked falls, liquids and haze.
- the same renderer runs in the editor, in q0player and in exports.

## plugins

- plugins are single `.qx` files written in q0ex. they can reach the project, the stage, the timeline, the selection and the settings, and add menu items, windows, keys and stage tools.
- **Plugins > Manage plugins** installs, switches and removes them. before installing, the editor shows the plugin's author, version and fingerprint, and asks.
- **Plugins > Plugin Studio** builds plugins from blocks (simple) or as text with completion and a reference (pro). examples are included.

## drawing

- gradients on every fill: the colour box of the fill, bucket, brushes, a selected shape and a vector asset switches between solid, linear and radial. stops are edited on a bar (click to add, drag to move, drag off to remove), with angle, size, reverse, even spacing and presets. new shapes, bucket fills and brush strokes take the gradient, fitted to each shape, and a solid bucket fill takes a gradient off again.
- the knife (**C**) cuts drawings apart along a dragged line, with an optional gap and a jagged crack.
- snapping (**M**) pulls points onto anchors, line ends, object origins and the stage's corners and middle.
- new lines are cut where they cross, so each piece can be picked and changed on its own. dragging the middle of a straight line bends it into an arc.
- **Make Variant** copies a symbol instance's symbol as a variant ("Pan ~2") to break or wound without touching the original; **<** and **>** step through the variants.
- **Modify > Strokes to Own Layer** moves a layer's outlines to a layer of their own.

## editor

- shortcuts work on Russian and other non-Latin keyboard layouts.
- every command can have several shortcuts, each removable, with a reset per command.
- the arrow-key nudge step, and its Shift step, are settings.
- several views of one document: **Layout > new panel** or **duplicate panel** shows two stages side by side, each with its own zoom, frame and selection.
- the editor uses less memory while idle: fallback fonts are no longer copied several times.
- copying in another program and pasting there no longer pastes into q0editor, and someone else's edit no longer stops playback.

## phones and tablets

- selecting something shows a bar over the canvas with copy, cut, paste, duplicate and delete, and **More** for paste in place, convert to symbol, break apart, flips, arrange, nudging and deselect.
- a layer's long-press menu copies, cuts, pastes and duplicates whole layers.
- the Back button clears the selection before it leaves the editor.

## q0player and export

- bitmaps are drawn with nearest sampling, so a moving sprite no longer blurs between whole pixels.
- mp4 exports on Windows include the timeline's audio.

## downloads

- Windows x64 and Windows on ARM (arm64) installers for q0editor and q0player.
- Android apks for q0editor and q0player (arm64).
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists every file's hash; `BUILD-INFO-windows-*.txt` holds the build id for bug reports.
