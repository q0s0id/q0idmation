# q0idmation v0.1.0-beta.6.2

beta.6.2 brings Animate-style layer parenting, with the camera taking part too. it adds a name-and-folder prompt for new symbols, particles made of your own symbols and drawings, and a reworked look for the editor. there are new chat and room tools online, a better 3D gizmo, shadows and text, Flash words for q0lang and a first Flash (.swf) importer.

## layer parenting

- a parenting view in the timeline, as in Animate: every layer gets a square in its outline colour. drag a square onto another layer to hang it there, drop it clear of the layers to free it, or click it for a menu. turn the view on with the button in the frame ruler's corner.
- a hung layer keeps its place, then moves, turns, scales and skews with its parent's object. its own keyframes and tweens stay relative to the parent, and chains are allowed. a link that would close a loop is refused.
- the camera takes part. hang a layer from the camera and it stays put in the camera's view: scores, titles, subtitles. hang the camera from a layer and it follows that layer's object while its own keys still pan, zoom and turn it.
- the selection box, its handles and the hit test follow a parented object, including right after its parent was moved.
- deleting a parent frees its children where they are, and one undo takes a link back.
- q0player plays parented layers, also inside symbols and on rigs.

## symbols and particles

- creating, converting or duplicating a symbol asks for its name (it starts as "Symbol N") and its library folder (the root, or the original's folder for a duplicate).
- symbols nest 16 deep instead of 8.
- the particle simulator can spawn a symbol or a drawing from the library instead of a plain shape.

## the editor's look

- redrawn icons and smoother, lighter panels, bars and tabs.
- gradients on the menu bar, panels, tabs and buttons, with ready-made ones in every theme. edit them in the theme editor like any other colour.
- light themes keep bold titles readable, and the menu bar's gradient no longer paints over its menus.
- keyboard shortcuts: a window to search every command and give it your own keys.
- interface sounds play on their own, so they never stop or restart timeline playback.

## 3D

- the move, turn and scale gizmo follows the pointer in depth. a ring seen edge-on still turns the way you drag, and dragging from the centre scales the whole object.
- 3D text is typed in place on the projected stage, with the caret where the letters are.
- shadows fit the objects that cast them, so they stay sharp.

## online

- chat settings: chat size on the canvas, history height, and notification sounds for room chat and direct messages, including your own sound file.
- search rooms by name. owners can close a room for everyone or delete it.
- big drawings load with much less memory.
- avatars move below a narrow timeline bar instead of covering its buttons.

## q0lang

- Flash words in `q0.timeline`: `stop!` and `play!` stop or play in place, and `gonext!` and `goprev!` step one frame. `rootrun!` and `rootstop!` drive the main timeline from any script, a symbol's too.
- frame labels resolve: `gorun! "intro"`.
- q0player sets Flash's `_currentframe` and `_totalframes` for the main timeline, for every object script, and as `name._currentframe` for named copies.

## importing Flash

- File > Import takes .swf files (Flash Player movies) as well as .fla and .xfl. art, symbols, timelines, masks, filters, sounds and text come in, and simple frame code becomes q0lang. code that cannot be translated yet is kept beside it as comments. this importer is new: whole games do not play yet.

## file compatibility

projects that use layer parenting are saved in a new format: **beta.6.1 and earlier cannot open them**, and only beta.6.2's q0player plays their .q0s movies. projects without parents stay readable by beta.6.1. beta.6.2 opens every older project.
