# q0bo

q0bo is q0editor's mascot: a red and black bot with an old LED screen for a face. He talks in lowercase old-internet speak and shows his mood on his screen.

## The first start

The very first time q0editor starts, q0bo pops up and offers the tutorial. **nah, skip** closes it, and he does not come back on his own. Either answer is remembered in the settings (`mascot.greeted`).

## The tutorial

**Help > Tutorial with q0bo** starts it again at any time.

The tutorial opens a new, unsaved project called "tutorial". It has two layers: "q0bo's stuff" for him and "ur stuff" for you.

q0bo has his own mouse pointer, a red arrow tagged "q0bo". With it he does everything for real in that project:
- He picks the oval tool and draws a ball.
- He drags a selection box around the ball and moves it.
- He clicks the last frame in the timeline and presses F6.
- He moves the ball on that keyframe, makes a motion tween and plays it.

The ball, the keyframe and the tween are ordinary project content, so undo works on them and you can save them.

Between his turns he asks you to do the same:
- draw a box
- move it
- stop and replay the animation

He notices when you have done it. Each of those steps also has **skip this 1**, and **[x] skip tutorial** in the corner of his bubble ends the whole thing.

While he talks:
- Click his bubble to show the rest of the line at once.
- Drag q0bo to move him and his bubble out of the way.
- Click him to poke him.
- Switch to another tab and he waits, offering to take you back.

His lines name your own shortcut keys. On phones the bits about keys are left out.

## ask q0bo

**Help > ask q0bo** opens his tab. It docks like any other panel, and the **Layout** menu lists it under the panels.

Type a question, like "how do i tween", or pick one of the topics. He answers in a few short steps. There are topics on:
- drawing and colours
- frames, tweens and easing
- layers, masks and symbols
- bones, the camera, 3D, lights, particles and physics
- scripts
- saving, exporting and importing

Where nothing docks (on phones, on Home and in script tabs), the tab opens as a window instead.
